# Veranda Website Plan

## Purpose

Reposition Veranda as a unified hardware and software platform that connects physical access points with digital systems.

Delivery will remain the primary market and clearest demonstration of the platform, while the site will leave room for other access-control and asset-management applications.

## Phase 1 Sitemap

### Home

Introduces the platform, demonstrates its components and capabilities, establishes its applications and impact, and directs visitors toward partnership or evaluation.

### Work With Us

Explains the different ways organizations can work with Veranda, including complete solutions, component integration, licensing, white-labelling, and other strategic relationships.

### Request Evaluation Kit

A dedicated page presented as the site’s primary call to action. It explains the purpose of the Evaluation Kit, who it is for, what organizations can evaluate, and how to begin a conversation with Veranda.

## Phase 2 Sitemap

- Our Team
- News

These pages can be added to the primary navigation once their content is ready.

# Homepage

## 1. Hero

Establish Veranda as an embedded access, monitoring, and automation platform.

The section should quickly communicate that Veranda combines physical hardware with connected software and can be integrated into partner products.

Primary actions:

- Explore the Platform
- Request an Evaluation Kit

## 2. Our Platform

Provide a high-level explanation of the unified Veranda platform and how it bridges physical and digital systems.

Introduce the relationship among:

- Locking hardware
- Modular circuit-board stack
- Sensors
- Wireless communications
- Cloud software
- Physical enclosure or access point

A platform graphic should help visitors understand how these elements operate as one system.

Introduce the platform’s four core capabilities:

- Authorization
- Auditability
- Automation
- Integration

## 3. Hardware

Explain the modular hardware architecture and demonstrate how different boards contribute to the complete system.

Retain and expand the existing scroll-based board-stack presentation. Each stage introduces one board and supporting contextual graphics.

### Primary Board

Coordinates the system through its primary processor and radios, consolidating information from the other boards and communicating through BLE and LoRaWAN.

### Sensor Board

Provides environmental and motion sensing, currently including air quality, temperature, and acceleration.

### Lock Board

Controls the locking unit, including its motor drivers and cam switches.

### Interaction Board

Manages buttons, LEDs, and other elements used for local interaction and system feedback.

### Power Board

Connects to the system’s batteries and distributes power across the hardware stack.

The purpose of this section is to demonstrate modularity and technical depth without turning the homepage into a technical specification sheet.

## 4. Software

Explain how Veranda’s software manages and extends the physical system.

Retain the current text-and-mobile-app presentation while describing the software’s primary responsibilities, such as:

- Managing access and permissions
- Scheduling access
- Monitoring devices and sensors
- Reviewing activity and events
- Receiving status updates and alerts
- Supporting users, operators, and partner workflows

The section should present the mobile app as one interface into the broader Veranda software platform.

## 5. Applications and Benefits

Merge the current Applications and Benefits sections.

Lead with Veranda’s primary delivery applications, followed by examples of how the platform can extend into other industries and physical-access scenarios.

The section should connect applications with the operational or social outcomes they may support, including:

- More flexible unattended delivery
- Reduced failed handoffs
- Reduced loss, theft, and spoilage
- Better access and condition records
- Improved delivery and field-service efficiency
- Reverse-logistics support
- Food-access and community-delivery programs
- Controlled access to spaces or assets

The purpose is to demonstrate breadth while keeping delivery clearly positioned as Veranda’s lead market.

## 6. Partnership and Integration

Provide a brief introduction to the ways organizations can work with Veranda.

Present Veranda as available through:

- A complete standalone solution
- Integration of selected hardware or software components
- Technology licensing
- White-labelled solutions
- Custom technical and product-development relationships

This section should direct visitors to the Work With Us page for more information.

## 7. Case Study: Dream Box

Demonstrate how Dion’s Chicago Dream is working with Veranda to develop Dream Box as a partner-led application of the platform.

The case study should show:

- The delivery challenge being addressed
- How Veranda technology supports the Dream Box
- How the solution fits Dion’s Chicago Dream’s operations
- The distinction between the partner’s product and Veranda’s underlying platform
- The intended value for the organization and the households it serves

The purpose is to make the licensing and white-label strategy tangible through a real relationship.

## 8. Delivering With Veranda

Merge the current McKinsey problem section with the “Optimize Your Last Mile Operations” section.

Use industry research to explain the cost and operational consequences of blind handoffs, including:

- Dwell time
- Failed deliveries
- Redelivery
- Loss and theft
- Coordination effort
- Inefficient use of vehicles and personnel

Connect these problems to the capabilities Veranda can bring to the physical handoff point.

This section should establish the market need and potential impact without presenting industry estimates as guaranteed Veranda results.

## 9. Final Call to Action

Adapt the existing “See the platform in motion” section.

Invite organizations to evaluate how Veranda could work within their product, delivery program, or access-control application.

Primary action:

- Request an Evaluation Kit

Secondary action, if needed:

- Work With Us

# Work With Us Page

## Introduction

Explain that Veranda can support organizations at different levels, from adopting a complete solution to integrating individual platform components.

## Ways to Work Together

Describe the principal engagement models:

- Complete Veranda solution
- Hardware integration
- Software integration
- Platform licensing
- White-labelling
- Custom development or co-development
- Strategic relationships

## Engagement Process

Provide a simple overview of how a relationship may progress:

- Initial discussion
- Requirements and fit assessment
- Evaluation
- Pilot
- Integration or deployment
- Longer-term commercial relationship

## Partnership Considerations

Identify the areas normally explored with a prospective partner, such as:

- Product and operational requirements
- Enclosure and hardware integration
- Sensors and connectivity
- Branding
- Software requirements
- Support responsibilities
- Commercial structure

## Call to Action

Direct qualified visitors toward the Evaluation Kit page or a preliminary partnership discussion.

# Request Evaluation Kit Page

## Introduction

Explain that the Evaluation Kit provides a practical way to explore the Veranda platform before committing to a pilot or integration.

## Purpose of the Kit

Describe the types of questions an organization can investigate, including:

- Hardware fit
- Access workflows
- Connectivity
- Sensor requirements
- Software capabilities
- User interaction
- Operational integration

## Who It Is For

Identify the general types of organizations and teams that may benefit from an evaluation without limiting the kit to a specific industry.

## Evaluation Process

Explain the broad progression from an initial meeting through evaluation, feedback, and possible pilot planning.

## Request Form

Collect enough information to understand:

- The organization
- The use case
- The physical product or access point
- The operational problem
- Evaluation goals
- Approximate timing

## Next Steps

Set expectations for what happens after a request is submitted, including a meeting with Veranda to assess fit and define an appropriate evaluation.

# Phase 1 Content Principles

- Present Veranda as a platform rather than only a delivery box.
- Use delivery as the lead market while leaving room for broader applications.
- Distinguish the Veranda reference enclosure from the underlying technology.
- Separate demonstrated capabilities from expected operational outcomes.
- Attribute external industry statistics.
- Qualify technical performance claims where results depend on configuration or environment.
- Keep the Evaluation Kit as the consistent primary call to action.
